Built for people who take deep work seriously.

⏱

Pomodoro Timer

Focus/break loop with long break support. Configurable durations, auto-starts on schedule.

🖥

System Tray

Lives in your tray with live timer. Single-click to pause, double-click to open. Always visible, never in the way.

📅

Scheduled Sessions

Set deep work times per weekday. Grassdoro auto-starts your session at the right time, every day.

🎵

Lofi Music

Plays during focus, stops on break. Bring your own tracks — just drop .mp3 files in the lofi folder.

🕌

Prayer Time

Auto-pauses at prayer times based on your city. Lockscreen reminder, then resumes where you left off.

📊

Stats & Streaks

GitHub-style contribution graph. Track your streak, total focus hours, and daily goal progress.

🔕

Do Not Disturb

Mutes OS notifications during focus sessions. Automatically re-enables on break.

🚀

Auto-launch

Starts with your computer. Your schedule kicks in automatically, no manual launch needed.
